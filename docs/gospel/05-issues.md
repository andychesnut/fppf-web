## What the issues list is

The issues list is where the business's real obstacles and open questions live, one level above execution. Execution work (features, bugs, tasks) flows through the Process (document 6). The issues list holds what must be decided or solved, worked **IDS: Identify, Discuss, Solve.**

## Where it lives

GitHub Issues on the FPPF repo is the tracker (ruling 2026-11-17, not Linear). Until issues are filed there, this document is the list.

## What earns the list

An issue belongs here if it meets any of these:
1. It blocks a rock.
2. It moves a scorecard metric, or explains why one is off-pace.
3. It requires a tradeoff between customer and business.
4. It touches the floor or a settled decision (sacred-cow watch).
5. It is a strategy question, including any market shift.

## Solved means

A shipped fix, a recorded decision, or a deliberate recorded choice to go another direction. Nothing is left stranded and nothing is silently deleted.

## Working an issue

1. **Identify:** file it with its evidence and the metric or rock it touches.
2. **Discuss:** the GM brings a recommendation in the brief. The Principal rules or redirects.
3. **Solve:** the ruling is logged in the Decisions document (document 8). The issue closes.
