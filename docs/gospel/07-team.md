# FPPF - The Team

**Status: DRAFT, 2026-11-17. Awaiting the Principal's ratification.**
Gospel document 7 of 8. Modeled on the skookr Team document. **No seat below is active.** Creating a seat always needs the Principal's approval (charter section 2). Seat names and personas are the Principal's to give, so seats are listed by role.

## The accountability chart

**The Principal (Andy)** decides: strategy, spend, everything public or user-bound outside ratified patterns, and how the product works and feels. **The GM** owns the two outcomes (business success and customer success, customer first) and runs the cadence (document 1). Seats execute.

Today the GM covers every lane directly. A seat is activated when a lane's workload or risk justifies a dedicated charter.

## Variants for the Principal to choose between

**Variant A: all six seats active.** Matches the studio. Highest coverage. Highest run cost for a $100 per month envelope, since each seat is an agent run with its own daily report.

**Variant B (GM recommendation): lean start with three seats.** Architect, Demand, and Domain. The GM covers Voice, Designer, and Financer directly until a lane needs its own seat. Domain is included because FPPF asserts financial facts and the legal floor is the biggest risk. Architect and Demand are the build and growth engines.

**Variant C: GM only.** No seats yet. Cheapest. Weakest separation of authorship from review, which the Process requires (**the author never certifies their own work**).
