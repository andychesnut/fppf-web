First Principles Money

Start with what you want. Work backwards.

Most money tools start with a budget. This starts with the life you want and works back to what has to be true, one step at a time, with every step shown.

Work yours through Free. Your answers stay in your browser.
An example with made-up numbers
1I want to build furniture full time.
2That life costs $5,000 a month. I could earn $2,000 of it while I get going.
3So each month would take $3,000 from savings. I will give it nine months at most: $27,000.
4I have $22,000 and keep $1,000 a month.
So I am 5 months away.
